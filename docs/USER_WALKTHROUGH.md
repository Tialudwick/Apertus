# Apertus User Walkthrough & Operational Guide

## 1. Public Searchers & Crisis Workers
* **Zero Registration:** Anyone can visit the site on mobile or desktop to view available beds instantly.
* **Filtering:** Filter by County, Region, Gender served, MARR Support Level (I-IV), or immediate availability.
* **Holding a Bed:**
  1. Click **Hold Bed for 24 Hours**.
  2. Enter client initials/alias (No personal health information required).
  3. The system holds the bed on standby for 24 hours with a live countdown timer.

## 2. Recovery Residence House Managers
* **Goal:** Update bed counts in **under 30 seconds**.
* **Steps:**
  1. Click **House Manager Portal** in the navigation bar.
  2. Select your recovery residence.
  3. Enter total bed capacity and currently available beds.
  4. Click **Save & Update Real-Time Availability**.
* **7-Day Rolling Window:** Houses updated within 7 days display a green verification badge (`Updated < 7 days ago`).

## 3. MARR Administrators
* **Directory Verification:** Admin accounts can toggle `is_marr_certified` status in the Supabase backend dashboard to prevent unverified operators from listing homes on the public tracker.
